ABOUT ME

I am a creative, innovative, and technology-driven individual with a strong passion for exploring different fields of art, music, design, and technology.

My creative interests include photography and videography, where I enjoy capturing moments, telling stories through visuals, and exploring different approaches to visual storytelling. I am also a graphic designer with an interest in creating visually appealing designs and communicating ideas through art.

Music is another important part of my journey. I am involved in music production, music and orchestra, and I play the trombone, euphonium, and tuba. I also have an interest in sound engineering, including sound recording, mixing, and audio production.

On the technology side, I am passionate about web development and programming. I enjoy exploring how websites and software are built and developing digital solutions.

My interests also extend to robotics and science fair engineering, where I enjoy innovation, experimentation, problem-solving, and bringing scientific and engineering ideas to life.

I believe in continuous learning, creativity, teamwork, and exploring new opportunities. I am always eager to collaborate with creatives, musicians, designers, developers, and innovators.

My goal is to keep expanding my knowledge, work on meaningful projects, and build a journey that brings together creativity, music, technology, and engineering.
